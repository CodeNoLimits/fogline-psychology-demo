I am Dr. Nora Ellery.

For twelve years I have worked with adults who look fine from the outside: they keep the job, the family and the calendar going, and they are worn out by it.

We start with what is hardest this month. I use cognitive and acceptance-based methods, explain why I suggest each step, and check with you every few weeks that it is helping.

  • PsyD, clinical psychology
  • Licensed in California, sample license PSY 00000
  • Office in Harbor Town, and telehealth across California
