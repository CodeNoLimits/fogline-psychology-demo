If you are a new client

  1. Book your first call

    We talk for 15 minutes and decide together whether to go on.

  2. Open your invitation

    The portal sends you an email with a personal link. Choose a password only you know.

  3. Fill in the forms

    Health history, consent and privacy notice. Plan about 20 minutes; you can save and come back.

  4. Upload earlier records, if you have any

    Use the portal upload. Please do not send them by email.

How this website protects you

No contact form
You are never asked to type health details here.
No advertising trackers
No advertising pixels and no visitor analytics.
No health details by email
Forms, records and messages stay in the portal.
Portal and scheduling
Both open in the practice’s own secure tools.

If you are coming back

I forgot my portal password

Choose “Forgot password” on the portal sign-in page. The reset link comes from the portal, never from this website.

I need to update my information

Sign in and open your profile. Changes reach the practice the same day.

I would like an estimate of costs

If you are not using insurance, you can receive a Good Faith Estimate in writing before your first session.
